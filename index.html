About Isabel

Warranted counsellor, Clinic Lead, and co-founder of Vita Mea

Isabel Camilleri has been practising as a counsellor in Malta since 2009. She works with adults, couples and families who want to understand what is happening between them — and inside themselves — without being rushed or judged.

She holds a Master's in Counselling from the University of Malta. Her research looked at what actually helps in therapy with distressed couples: not a single technique, but the quality of the relationship, the therapist's presence, and a couple's willingness to try something different together.

  • Warranted counsellor and member of the Malta Association for the Counselling Profession.
  • Special interest in couples, blended families, anxiety, grief and corporate wellbeing.
  • Sees clients in person at Vita Mea Clinic in Mosta, the practice she co-founded.

How sessions feel

Isabel's approach is integrative and relational. Sessions are not a script. They are a confidential conversation that helps you notice patterns, name what hurts, and find a pace of change that can last.

That might mean sitting with a couple who keep circling the same fight. It might mean helping a family make room for a new step-parent, or walking with someone through anxiety or bereavement. The work is tailored — not one-size-fits-all.

An integrative, relational approach

Isabel does not ask you to fit a model. She draws on integrative counselling and relational therapy so the work can follow what you actually need — insight, practical tools, or simply a place where you do not have to hold it together.

Confidential

Sessions are private and guided by professional ethics and data-protection standards.

Unhurried

Individual sessions are typically 50 minutes. Couples and family sessions usually run longer.

In person in Mosta

Most work happens at Vita Mea Clinic, with online sessions when travel is genuinely difficult.

Human

You do not need a diagnosis or a GP letter to begin. Curiosity and a willingness to talk are enough.

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before you book counselling in Malta.

Where does Isabel see clients?

In person at Vita Mea Clinic on Triq Sir Temi Zammit, Żokrija, Mosta. Online sessions can be arranged when attending the clinic is genuinely difficult.

How do I book a first session?

Send an enquiry through the Vita Mea contact form or call +356 9985 4942. Mention that you would like to work with Isabel Camilleri. The clinic aims to reply within two working days.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can contact the clinic directly. A GP letter is welcome if you already have one, but it is never required to start counselling.

What does a first session involve?

It is a getting-to-know-you meeting. Isabel will ask what brings you in, a little of the background, and what you hope might change. You do not have to share more than you are ready for — and it is also your chance to see whether the fit feels right.

How much does counselling cost?

Fees depend on the type of session — individual, couples, family or organisational work. Vita Mea Clinic confirms the fee with you before you book, so there are no surprises.

Is counselling confidential?

Yes. What you share stays in the room, except in rare situations where there is a serious risk of harm. Those limits are explained clearly at the start.
